# GDPR Compliance Notice

**For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and United Kingdom**

## Data Controller

The parent device operator acts as the data controller for the child's notification data. Sync Guardian acts as a data processor.

## Lawful Basis for Processing

We process personal data based on:
- **Consent**: Explicit consent from parents for children's data
- **Legitimate interests**: Providing the notification mirroring service

## Data Processing Summary

| Processing Activity | Data Categories | Purpose |
|--------------------|----------------|---------|
| Account creation | Email, name | Authentication |
| Device pairing | Device IDs | Secure connection |
| Notification mirroring | Notification content | Service delivery |
| Push notifications | FCM tokens | Alert delivery |

## Your Rights (Articles 15-22)

- Right to access (Article 15)
- Right to rectification (Article 16)
- Right to erasure / "right to be forgotten" (Article 17)
- Right to restrict processing (Article 18)
- Right to data portability (Article 20)
- Right to object (Article 21)
- Rights related to automated decision-making (Article 22)

## Data Transfers

Data is stored on US-based servers (Supabase). Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) are in place.

## International Transfer

Your information may be transferred to and maintained on servers located outside your country. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

## Data Retention

Personal data is retained as follows:
- Account data: Until account deletion
- Notification data: 30 days
- Session data: Until session expiry or logout

## Supervisory Authority

You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
