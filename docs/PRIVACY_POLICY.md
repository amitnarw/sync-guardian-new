# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Sync Guardian ("we," "our," or "us") operates a mobile application that allows parents to monitor notifications on their child's Android device. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
- Email address (for authentication)
- Display name and profile image (optional)

### 2.2 Device Information
- Device model, operating system version, and app version
- Device identifiers (UUID generated by the app)
- Push notification tokens (FCM)
- Notification listener status and battery optimization status

### 2.3 Notification Data
- App names and package names from which notifications originate
- Notification titles, body text, and timestamps
- Notification metadata

### 2.4 Usage Data
- App foreground/background state
- Last-seen timestamps
- Pairing event data

## 3. How We Use Information

- To establish and maintain a secure connection between parent and child devices
- To mirror notifications from child device to parent device
- To send push notifications to parent devices
- To improve app functionality and user experience
- To comply with legal obligations

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- Data is stored on Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security
- All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
- Authentication is handled via Supabase Auth (JWT-based)
- We implement industry-standard security measures

## 5. Data Retention

- Notification records are retained for 30 days
- Account data is retained until account deletion
- Pairing data is retained until pairing is revoked
- Device tokens are updated on each app launch

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share data:
- With service providers (Supabase, Firebase Cloud Messaging)
- When required by law
- To protect our rights and safety

## 7. Parental Consent

For users under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction), we require verifiable parental consent before collecting data. The parent device operator serves as the data controller for the child's data.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your data
- Export your data
- Withdraw consent
- Object to processing

## 9. Children's Privacy

This app is designed for family use. We comply with:
- COPPA (US): Parental consent required for under-13 users
- GDPR-K (EU): Parental consent required for under-16 users (varies by member state)
- Age-appropriate design code (UK)
