# COPPA Compliance Notice

**For users in the United States**

## Parental Consent Requirements

Sync Guardian complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If you are a parent or guardian, you must provide verifiable parental consent before your child (under 13) uses this application.

## What We Collect from Children

- Device identifiers
- Notification content from the child's device
- App usage metadata
- Device status information

## How We Use Children's Data

The data is used exclusively to provide the notification mirroring service to you, the parent. We do not:
- Sell children's data
- Use children's data for advertising
- Share children's data with third parties (except service providers)

## Parental Rights

As a parent, you may:
- Review the child's data
- Request deletion of the child's data
- Refuse further collection
- Revoke consent at any time

## Consent Mechanism

When setting up a child device, you will be prompted to confirm:
1. You are the parent or legal guardian
2. You authorize the collection of notification data from the child's device
3. You understand the purpose and scope of the monitoring
